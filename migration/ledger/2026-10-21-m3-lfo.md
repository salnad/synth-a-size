## 2026-10-21: M3 PR 2, `sas-modulation` LFO family; keytrack drift needs an owner decision

- **Ported** (`port/crates/modulation`):
  - `synth_lfo.rs`: `vital::SynthLfo` (`modulators/synth_lfo.cpp`). It
    covers every sync type at both rates (trigger, sync, envelope, sustain
    envelope, loop point, loop hold), plus delay, fade, smoothing, stereo
    phase and `correctToTime`. The shape is a shared
    `Rc<RefCell<sas_common::LineGenerator>>`.
  - `lfo_module.rs`: `vital::LfoModule`, with registry controls and
    `createTempoSyncSwitch` (free, tempo, dotted, triplet, keytrack).
- **Captures** (`migration/reference/src/modules/modulation/`):
  - `synth_lfo.cpp` and `lfo_module.cpp`. A shared `line_shape.h` builds
    the shape from `params.shape`: a named `init*` or custom
    points/powers/smooth. The test harness mirrors it.
  - `osc_frequency` (Hz) is captured as SynthLfo's per-sample phase
    increment, `frequency * (1.0f / getSampleRate())`, like
    `op_tempo_chooser_phase_increment`.
  - `synth_lfo.json` has 36 cases. They cover:
    - each sync type at both rates;
    - delay and fade, including the maximums (4 s, 8 s);
    - smoothing at 2^4 s and below the minimum half-life;
    - stereo phase and custom (smooth) shapes;
    - 2^-7 and 2^9 Hz;
    - modulated frequency (2^-7 to 2^9 Hz), phase, stereo and fade;
    - blocks of 1, 37 and 128; 48 and 96 kHz; oversample 2.
  - `lfo_module.json` has 21 live cases: defaults, free frequency,
    frequency extremes, tempo/dotted/triplet sync, tempo extremes,
    keytrack (control rate, and the low band at both rates) and smooth
    time.
  - All use the `module_defaults` tolerances.

- **Open: audio-rate keytrack at high notes (owner decision needed).**
  Three cases cannot pass faithfully. I moved them to
  `migration/corpus/modules/modulation/pending/lfo_module.json`, which
  the harness does not load. No tolerance was changed.
  - The cases:
    - `modulation/lfo_module/keytrack_audio`: notes 60 and 96 →
      `value` 4.2e-3, −57.5 dB;
    - `modulation/lfo_module/keytrack_range_audio`: MIDI 0→127,
      transpose +36, tune +1 → `value` 4.7e-3, −56.8 dB; `osc_phase`
      0.90, −29.6 dB;
    - `modulation/lfo_module/keytrack_range_control`: same inputs at
      control rate → `value` 4.5e-3, −56.7 dB; `osc_phase` 0.90,
      −29.6 dB.
  - **The port output is bit-identical to the `REF_FAST_MATH=OFF`
    reference** on these cases. The proxy fails the same way, so the CI
    tolerance-calibration job would fail too.
  - Cause: the fast-math reference's keytrack frequency (TempoChooser,
    MIDI → Hz) differs by about 1e-7 relative. `osc_frequency` itself
    passes (2.6e-8 and 1.7e-6 as phase increment). At audio-rate LFO
    frequencies (hundreds of Hz to tens of kHz), that difference
    accumulates into phase drift over the 1 s render. `osc_phase`
    differs by 0.9 where the encoded phase wraps.
  - There is no normalized form that removes accumulated phase drift. The
    passing band stays live (`keytrack_control`, `keytrack_low_band_*`:
    notes about 0 and 16 at transpose −48).
  - Status: the coordinator has escalated this to the owner as one rule
    that also covers comb, reverb, the crossover and oscillator pitch
    sweeps. The rule is exact comparison against the no-fast-math
    reference, allowed only where CI shows the official reference fails
    the limits against its own no-fast-math build. These three cases
    qualify. They stay parked until the rule lands, with no phase-blind
    metrics and no tolerance change.
