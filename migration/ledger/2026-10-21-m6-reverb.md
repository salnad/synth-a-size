## 2026-10-21: M6 PR 2, reverb (`sas-effects`); reverb captures exceed the module gates on fast-math noise alone

Ported `effects/reverb.{h,cpp}` (`Reverb`: the 16-line feedback delay
network, allpass diffusion, shelving filters, chorus drift and pre-delay) and
`modules/reverb_module.{h,cpp}`. Per-ISA sites: the feedback matrix's
`poly_float::sum()` (A4) and the pre-delay's `StereoMemory::get` (A1a), both
in `sas-simd`/`sas-dsp-core`.

Upstream quirk kept (and covered): inside the sample loop only the high-shelf
coefficient and amplitude, the decays, the chorus amount, wet/dry and the
pre-delay ramp advance; the pre-filter and low-shelf coefficients and the low-shelf
amplitude use the previous block's values. In the first block both
pre-filters therefore run with the constructor's 0.1 coefficient and cancel
exactly, so an impulse inside the first block never reaches the network. The
impulse cases put their impulse after the first block.

Captures: `effects/reverb` (13 cases: 8 s and 10 s impulse tails, decay
0.05–100 s, size 0–1, chorus amount 0–1 and frequency 0–16 Hz, every filter
cutoff 0–130 and gain −24–0 dB, pre-delay 0–0.3 s and clamped at 4.5 s, wet
0–1, 48/96/192 kHz, 2× oversampling) and `effects/reverb_module` (8 cases:
every control over its parameter range, enable toggling, 96 kHz, 2×
oversampling, a 10 s maximum-decay tail).

**The port is bit-identical to the `REF_FAST_MATH=OFF` proxy on all 21
outputs** (`max_abs_error` 0, residual −inf). Against the fast-math
reference, 13 outputs miss the module defaults (1e-4, −80 dB), and the proxy
misses them by the same amounts, so CI's `tolerance-calibration` job fails on
the same cases. The feedback network amplifies the reference's reassociation
noise, and the pre-delay position is an f32 accumulator near 10^4 samples, so
a few-ULP difference moves the interpolated read. That is the "feedback
chaos" the module gates were calibrated without
(`decisions/reference-determinism.md`, "Module tolerances").

**A4 is not visible at these gates** (review note): using NEON's
`poly_float::sum()` order on x86 moves the live reverb outputs by at most
4.8e-7 against the no-fast-math build, below the ~9e-5 fast-math noise. A4
therefore rests on `sas-simd`'s own tests until an exact no-fast-math
comparison covers these cases.

**Interim coverage** (cases named `interim_*`, reviewer request): 0.25 s
versions of the parked cases, short enough that the network has not
amplified the reference's rounding noise past the gates: `effects/reverb`
gets an impulse tail, decay 0.05→100, size 0→1, chorus amount and

192 kHz (8 cases); `effects/reverb_module` gets decay and size, filters and
