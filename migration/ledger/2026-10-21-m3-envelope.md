## 2026-10-21: M3 PR 1, `sas-modulation` envelope family

- **Ported** (`port/crates/modulation`):
  - `envelope.rs`: `vital::Envelope`, both rates (`modulators/envelope.cpp`);
  - `envelope_module.rs`: `vital::EnvelopeModule`, with quartic time
    controls and base power controls built through `SynthModule`
    (`modules/envelope_module.cpp`).
- **Crate setup:**
  - depends on `sas-simd`, `sas-common` and `sas-dsp-core`;
  - the `aarch64-semantics` feature forwards to `sas-simd` and `sas-common`;
  - the macro, mod wheel, pitch wheel, velocity, aftertouch and note
    sources are built by `synth_voice_handler.cpp`, not by the M3 modules,
    so they stay with the voice handler (M7). The crate docs say so.
- **Captures** (`migration/reference/src/modules/modulation/`):
  - `envelope.cpp` is unchanged. `envelope.json` keeps `adsr` and adds 18
    cases: control and audio rate; delay and hold; powers at ±20; zero
    times; the maximum times (delay/hold 4 s, attack/decay/release 32 s);
    retrigger, kill and per-voice triggers; per-block modulated inputs;
    blocks of 1, 37 and 128; 48 and 96 kHz; oversample 2.
  - New `envelope_module.cpp`: the factory calls `init()` as a parent
    router would, then sets `params.controls` on the module's controls.
    `envelope_module.json` has 5 cases: defaults, a DAHDSR at both rates,
    and the registry range extremes at both rates.
  - All new cases use the `module_defaults` tolerances. `adsr` keeps its
    stricter 1e-5 / −100 dB.
- **Test harness** (`port/crates/modulation/tests/common/`):
  - a module table mirrors the C++ registrations;
  - `run_module` drives the processors;
  - every output goes through `assert_parity!`;
  - the global seed counter is reset from the spec's `seed`, and tests
    are serialized around it.
  - `parameters.rs` is a test-only copy of the env/lfo/random/modulation
    rows of `synth_parameters.cpp`, because `sas-preset` has not merged.
    A wrong row would change the captured output.
- **Metrics (x86_64 reference):**
  - 48 outputs in 24 cases, 44 of them bit-identical;
  - worst max_abs_error 1.13e-6, worst residual −137.1 dB, both on
    `modulation/envelope/max_powers_control_rate:value`;
  - worst max_abs_error is on `modulated_inputs_control_rate:value`
    (1.13e-6, −140.3 dB);
  - the no-fast-math proxy passes all 48 outputs, worst 1.13e-6 /
    −140.3 dB on that same case.
- Hashes for the x86_64 identity come from this PR's CI
  `reference-renders` artifact (run 39521472191). They are identical to
  the ones first written from a local CI-identity tree. macOS entries
  follow once PR #2 merges.
