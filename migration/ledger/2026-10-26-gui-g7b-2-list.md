## 2026-10-26: GUI G7b (2), the preset list, search and favourites

The second of three preset browser PRs, on G7b-1 (#157): the list's rows,
sorting, search, style filters and favourites, a shared text editor and
scroll bar, and a generated data folder both renderers read. The folder list
and the row menu come in G7b-3.

**The list** (`sections/preset_browser.rs`, `PresetList`)

- Every preset under the data folder's Presets folders and its banks'
  (`preset_files::all_files`, `LoadSave::getAllPresets`), read again when an
  editor window opens, as upstream builds the list with the editor.
- Each row: the star (filled in the primary colour for a favourite, else
  outlined at half the text colour's alpha), name, style (capitalised from
  the lower-cased cache), author and creation date ("26 Oct 2026"), in the
  light face at half the row height, with ellipses
  (`loadBrowserCache`); the selected row and the hovered row get additive
  highlights (`renderOpenGlComponents`).
- Authors and styles are read as `LoadSave::getAuthorFromFile` and
  `getStyleFromFile` do: the author's fast path for files starting
  `{"author":"` (40 characters at most), the style from the first 5000
  bytes (none for smaller files). Dates are the file's creation time (the
  birth time on macOS, the status change time on Linux, as JUCE reads them)
  as a local date.
- A click on a column title sorts by it, again reverses it; the sort is
  stable and in place, as `Array::sort(comparator, true)`: names, styles and
  authors in natural order ignoring case, favourites first, and "ascending"
  dates newest first (`FileDateAscendingComparator`).
- The search box and the style buttons filter: every word of the search in
  the name or author, and the style among those on (`filter`). Showing or
  hiding the browser empties the search (`visibilityChanged`).
- A click on a row loads it; a click on its star marks or unmarks a
  favourite, saved in a `favorites` file beside the config
  (`LoadSave::addFavorite`). Wheel and scroll bar scroll the rows.
- Keys, while the search box doesn't have the keyboard: up or left and
  down or right load the previous or next preset, Escape hides the browser.
  In the search box Escape clears it and keeps the keyboard, as JUCE's
  `TextEditor` does (the review note on #157).

**The header's arrows** (`PresetBrowser::jumpToPreset`)

- G7a stepped through the active file's folder. Upstream does that only
  for an external preset (Open External, Export); after a pick from the
  list, Initialize Preset, or at startup, the arrows move the list's
  selection, wrapping round. With nothing selected the index starts at
  -1, so previous goes to the second last, as upstream's does.

**Shared controls**

- `controls::text_editor` is `OpenGlTextEditor` on one line: the skin's
  background and border, the light face at half its height inside JUCE's
  indents, the prompt while empty and unfocused, and Escape keeping focus.
  A click asks the editor window for keyboard focus, as the chat's fields
  do, when the chat's focus setting (or its default) says so. G7c's save
  fields use it too. The FL checklist's keyboard item now includes the
  search box.
- `controls::scroll_bar` is the Effects tab's `OpenGlScrollBar` and
  `ScrollBar` behaviour, moved out for the list; the Effects tab uses it
  unchanged.
- `EditorModel::favourites` / `set_favourite`.

**Harness and corpus**

- `migration/corpus/generate.py` writes `migration/corpus/gui/data`: six
  authored presets with styles and authors, one in a folder inside Presets
  and one in a bank's folder (no factory content).
- `ref-gui --data-dir` sets upstream's data folder before the interface is
  built (`LoadSave::saveDataDirectory`, into the temporary HOME's config);
  `gui_reference.py` passes the corpus folder on every run, and the gallery's
  model uses it as its data directory.

**Tests**

- `preset_files`: authors (both paths, the 40-character cut, small files),
  styles (the 5000-byte rule), every preset under the root and its banks,
  and dates.
- `config::favourites_round_trip`.
- `tests/preset_selector.rs`: `the_list_sorts_by_the_clicked_column`,
  `styles_and_the_search_filter_the_list` (with Escape in the search box),
  `a_row_loads_and_its_star_marks_a_favourite`,
  `the_arrows_step_through_an_external_presets_folder` and
  `without_an_external_preset_the_arrows_step_through_the_list` (replacing

- Side by side with ref-gui on the corpus folder, the rows match in order and
  content; the folder list's rows are G7b-3's.

No audio change.
