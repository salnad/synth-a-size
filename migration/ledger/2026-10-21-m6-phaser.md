## 2026-10-21: M6 PR 5, phaser (`sas-effects`)

Ported `effects/phaser.{h,cpp}` (`Phaser`: a clean `PhaserFilter` from
`sas-filters` whose cutoff a triangle LFO sweeps around a center note; the
LFO phase is a wrapping `poly_int`) and `modules/phaser_module.{h,cpp}`.
Per-ISA sites: the phase and phase-offset conversions use `utils::toInt`
(A1), as does `PhaserFilter`'s coefficient lookup (A1c, `sas-filters`).
The phaser processor capture calls `init()` in its factory, as
`PhaserModule::init` does.

Captures: `effects/phaser` (11 cases: rate 1/32→4 Hz and 80 Hz, center
8→136 at audio rate, feedback 0→1, depth 0→48 with phase offset 0→1, blend
0→2, mix 0→1, 48/96 kHz, 2× oversampling; outputs `audio` and `cutoff`) and
`effects/phaser_module` (10 cases: free frequency −5→2, tempo 0→10, dotted
and triplet, center and depth, feedback/blend/offset, wet, enable toggling,
96 kHz, 2× oversampling). All under `module_defaults`.

Metrics (x86_64): 32 outputs pass; all 11 `cutoff` outputs are
bit-identical. Worst `max_abs_error` 6.1e-6 (`phaser/feedback_sweep`), worst
residual −114.5 dB (`phaser_module/feedback_blend_offset_sweep`). The
no-fast-math proxy passes every case.

Built on `PhaserFilter` from #15, which is on main.
