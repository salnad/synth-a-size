## 2026-10-27: GUI G4b-3c, aux connections: dragging a source onto an amount knob

**Changed.** Upstream's aux connections in `ModulationManager`: a source
dropped on another connection's amount knob modulates that connection's
amount.

- **The links** (`aux_connections_from_to_` and `aux_connections_to_from_`)
  come from the routes each frame, as `initAuxConnections` rebuilds them
  from the bank in slot order. A slot whose destination is
  `modulation_N_amount` links to slot N. A slot on its own amount links
  nothing. They are `AuxLinks` in `modulation.rs`.
- **Aux knobs** (`setAux`). The knob of a slot whose amount another slot
  modulates drives the end of its chain, at half its shown value per step
  (`getAuxMultiplier`, `sliderValueChanged`), so it shows the driven
  amount doubled each step.
  - It reads in the units of the destination at the chain's other end,
    times 2 per step (`setModulationSliderScale`).
  - Its centre dot is drawn in `kModulationMeterControl` on
    `kWidgetBackground` (`setModulationAmount(1)` → `modulation_amount_`).
  - New `Slider::value_scale` holds the share. The drag, its hold, double
    click, the popup and the text entry read and write the host through it.
  - The knob takes a different instance id, so it never shares one with
    the driven slot's own knob.
  - Only the amount and hover knobs get the dot (`addAuxConnection` calls
    `setAux` on those two). A selected knob drives the chain the same way
    without it.
  - A cycle of amounts, which a preset can hold, would hang upstream's
    walks. Here each walk stops before the first slot it would revisit
    (D27).
- **The drop** (`modulationDraggedToHoverSlider`). It works on any amount
  knob except a selected knob of the dragged source (`isCurrentModulator`
  stops the drag's handling for that frame) and an aux knob (`hasAux`).
  - The knob's connection goes to amount 0, and a new connection from the
    dragged source to that slot's amount gets half the knob's value,
    unipolar.
  - A knob is checked before the controls under it (`getComponentAt`).
  - Leaving the knob mid-drag (`clearTemporaryHoverModulation` →
    `removeModulation`) disconnects it and gives the knob's connection
    twice the aux amount back, which is its amount before.
  - As D18 requires, all of this reaches the engine only while dragging
    (`EditHandle::set_connection_values`, connect and disconnect). On
    release the host hears the new slot's values and the emptied amount,
    one gesture each. Leaving the knob writes nothing.
- **The editing amount overlay** on an aux knob draws its shown amount
  (`slider->getValue()`), twice its aux slot's, with its own slot's
  bipolar.
- **Knobs during a drag** show the values the host will hear on release.
  This covers an aux knob and the knob of a connection the drag just made
  beside its destination.
- **New GUI preset and view `modulations_aux`:** LFO 1 on oscillator 1's
  level at 0, with Env 2 on that slot's amount at 0.3.
